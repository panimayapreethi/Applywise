        About ApplyWise

        ApplyWise is a Career Application Tracking & Analytics Portal designed to help job seekers organize and monitor applications submitted across LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed, Glassdoor, referrals, and company career portals.

        The platform centralizes application records, tracks recruitment progress, and provides analytical insights to support data-driven career decisions.

        Developed by Preethi as an academic project to address the challenge of managing applications across multiple job platforms.
